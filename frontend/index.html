About Recycling

Recycling turns materials that would otherwise become waste into valuable resources. It conserves raw materials, saves energy, reduces greenhouse gas emissions, and helps protect natural habitats. Every small action adds up — your recycling choices make a measurable difference.

Why recycling matters
  • Reduces the amount of waste sent to landfills and incinerators.
  • Saves energy — manufacturing from recycled materials often uses less energy than using virgin raw materials.
  • Conserves natural resources such as timber, water and minerals.
  • Helps reduce pollution and greenhouse gas emissions.

Quick tips
  • Keep recyclables clean and dry — contamination can spoil whole batches.
  • Check local guidelines — accepted items differ by area.
  • Break down bulky cardboard to save space.
  • When in doubt, look up your local authority or use our support chat.

How to recycle — 4 simple steps
  1. Prepare: empty, rinse and dry containers where possible.
  2. Sort: separate paper, plastic, glass and metal according to your local rules.
  3. Request collection: schedule a pickup through your account dashboard or drop items at a local center.
  4. Track impact: use the dashboard to see how much you've diverted from landfill.
