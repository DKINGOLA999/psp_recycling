About Sigma Recycling

We make waste collection simple, transparent and responsible — connecting residents and businesses with reliable recycling services.

Our Mission

To reduce landfill waste by making recycling accessible and convenient for everyone. We help households and companies schedule pickups, report issues, and pay for services using a simple web interface.

  • Reliable scheduled pickups
  • Transparent payment and receipts
  • Clear communication with our support team

How the Website Works

Create an account, request a pickup or make a payment. Our admin team reviews requests and schedules collections. You will be notified when your payment or pickup is approved.

Use the Dashboard to manage your profile, view outstanding balances, and track past payments and pickup requests.

Waste Management & Recycling — Why it matters

Proper waste management conserves resources, reduces pollution, and lowers greenhouse gas emissions. Recycling materials like plastic, paper, metal and glass saves energy and reduces the need for raw materials.

Plastic

Rinse and sort by type when possible.

Paper

Keep dry; flatten boxes.

Metal

Empty cans and remove food residue.

Organic

Compost where possible; separate liquids.

Frequently Asked Questions

Sign in, go to Dashboard → Request Pickup. Choose a date and provide details; our team will confirm and schedule the collection.

Payments are handled through the payment page; currently we support bank transfer instructions entered in the form. Contact support for other options.

You will receive in-app notifications on the Dashboard when your payment or pickup status changes. We may also send email notifications if you provided a contact email.

Get Involved

Small actions add up. Reduce waste at home by buying less packaged goods, reusing containers, and separating recyclables. If you have questions, reach out — we're here to help.
